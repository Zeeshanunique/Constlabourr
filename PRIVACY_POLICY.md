# Privacy Policy for ConstLabour

**Last Updated:** December 25, 2024

## Introduction

ConstLabour ("we," "our," or "us") operates the ConstLabour mobile application (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service and the choices you have associated with that data.

By using our Service, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, do not use our Service.

## Information We Collect

### Personal Information

We collect several types of information to provide and improve our Service:

#### Account Information
- **Email address** - Required for account creation and authentication
- **Password** - Encrypted and stored securely for account access
- **Full name** - Required for user identification
- **User role** - Contractor, Foreman, or Labour (to determine access levels)

#### Worker Information (for Labour users)
- **Name** - Full name of the worker
- **Email address** - Contact email
- **Phone number** - Contact phone number
- **Aadhaar number** - Hashed and stored securely for identity verification (optional)
- **Bank account details** - For payroll processing (account number, IFSC code, bank name)
- **Skill level** - Professional skill classification
- **Daily wage** - Wage rate information
- **Mess charge** - Accommodation charges (if applicable)
- **Site assignment** - Construction site assignment information

#### Foreman Information
- **Name** - Full name of the foreman
- **Email address** - Contact email
- **Phone number** - Contact phone number
- **Site assignments** - Assigned construction sites

#### Site Information
- **Site name** - Name of the construction site
- **Address** - Physical address of the site
- **City and State** - Location information
- **Geographic location** - GPS coordinates (if location services are enabled)

#### Attendance Data
- **Check-in and check-out times** - Timestamp of attendance
- **Date** - Date of attendance
- **Status** - Present, Absent, or Leave status
- **Site ID** - Associated construction site

#### Payroll Information
- **Wage calculations** - Daily wages, deductions, and net pay
- **Payment status** - Paid or pending status
- **Date ranges** - Payroll period information

#### Agreement Data
- **Agreement terms** - Terms and conditions of employment
- **Digital signatures** - Captured signatures for agreement acceptance
- **Agreement status** - Acceptance status and dates

### Automatically Collected Information

- **Device information** - Device type, operating system, and app version
- **Usage data** - App interactions, features used, and session duration
- **Authentication tokens** - Stored securely for session management

## How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

1. **Service Provision**
   - To create and manage user accounts
   - To authenticate users and maintain secure sessions
   - To provide role-based access to features

2. **Labour Management**
   - To manage worker, foreman, and contractor profiles
   - To track attendance and work hours
   - To process payroll and manage payments
   - To manage construction site information

3. **Communication**
   - To send important notifications about your account
   - To communicate about attendance, payroll, and site updates

4. **Legal Compliance**
   - To comply with applicable laws and regulations
   - To enforce our terms of service
   - To protect our rights and prevent fraud

5. **Service Improvement**
   - To analyze usage patterns and improve app functionality
   - To fix bugs and enhance user experience

## Data Storage and Security

### Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using:

- **Supabase** - Cloud-based backend service for authentication and database storage
- **Expo Secure Store** - Encrypted local storage for sensitive authentication tokens
- **AsyncStorage** - Local storage for app preferences and cached data

### Security Measures

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information:

- **Encryption** - All data transmitted between the app and servers is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS
- **Password Security** - Passwords are hashed using secure algorithms before storage
- **Authentication** - Multi-factor authentication support through Supabase
- **Access Controls** - Role-based access controls limit data access to authorized users only
- **Secure Storage** - Sensitive data is stored using encrypted storage mechanisms

### Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to:
- Provide our services to you
- Comply with legal obligations
- Resolve disputes and enforce agreements

When you delete your account, we will delete or anonymize your personal information, except where we are required to retain it for legal or regulatory purposes.
