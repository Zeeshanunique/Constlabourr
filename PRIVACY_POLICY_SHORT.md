# Privacy Policy - ConstLabour

**Last Updated:** December 25, 2024

## Overview

ConstLabour collects and uses personal information to provide construction labour management services. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

## Data We Collect

- **Account Information:** Email, password (encrypted), name, user role
- **Worker Data:** Name, contact info, Aadhaar (hashed), bank details, wages, attendance
- **Site Information:** Location, address, GPS coordinates (if enabled)
- **Attendance & Payroll:** Check-in/out times, payment records, wage calculations
- **Agreements:** Digital signatures, terms acceptance

## How We Use Data

- Provide and manage the Service
- Process payroll and track attendance
- Manage worker, foreman, and site information
- Comply with legal obligations
- Improve app functionality

## Data Storage

Data is stored securely using:
- **Supabase** - Cloud backend (authentication & database)
- **Encrypted local storage** - For authentication tokens
- All data transmission is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS)

## Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including encryption, secure password hashing, and role-based access controls.

## Third-Party Services

We use **Supabase** (https://supabase.com) for backend services. See their privacy policy: https://supabase.com/privacy

## Your Rights

You can access, update, or delete your data through the app. Contact us to exercise your rights or request data deletion.

## Data Retention

We retain data as long as necessary to provide services or comply with legal obligations. You can delete your account at any time.

## Children's Privacy

Our Service is not intended for users under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
